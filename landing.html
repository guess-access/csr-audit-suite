📊 Evaluation Metrics

Section 1: Call Flow (20 pts)

Proper dealership branding announcement and agent self-introduction

Section 2: Body of Call (24 pts)

Reason for transfer, customer name collection, department introduction

Section 3: System Process (21 pts)

Warm transfer protocol (3-ring hold), data entry accuracy, correct disposition selection

Section 4: Management Skills (35 pts)

Communication clarity, empathy, active listening, tone, efficiency, and confidence

Section 5: Zero Tolerance (Auto-Fail)

No rudeness/profanity, no policy violations, no intentional call avoidance

Total Score: 0-100

85+ Passing (QA form standard) | 70-84 Coaching Required | Below 70 Needs Work | Auto-Fail = 0% + PIP review
